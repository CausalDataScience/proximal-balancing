\section{Proof Obligations}
\label{app:proofs}

The first proof obligation is a transfer result from an observable joint balance condition to a latent balance condition. Its assumptions must state the proxy measurement relation and the injectivity or completeness property used in the transfer.

The second proof obligation is identification of $\tau$ from the observed data distribution after latent balance has been established. The proof must list every causal assumption and show where each assumption enters the identifying functional.

The third proof obligation is a boundary construction. It must exhibit two latent data-generating processes that agree on the relevant observed information but disagree on the target whenever a required transfer assumption is removed.
