\section{Identification Theory}
\label{sec:identification}

\subsection{Causal and Proxy Assumptions}

The canonical results use the following assumptions, separated by their logical roles. Consistency states $Y=Y(A)$, and latent conditional exchangeability states
\begin{align}
Y(a)\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid(X,U),
\qquad a\in\{0,1\}.
\end{align}
The pretreatment proxy channel is treatment-invariant:
\begin{align}
W(a)=W,
\qquad
W\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid(X,U).
\end{align}
For each $x$, let $K_x(\cdot\mid u)$ denote the conditional law of $W$ given $X=x$ and $U=u$. Conditional mixture injectivity means that equality of two mixtures $\int K_x(\cdot\mid u)dQ(u)$ implies equality of their admissible mixing laws $Q$. This condition is used only to interpret exact balance as full latent balance.

Every retained stratum must have positive mass and both treatment arms. Specifically, for constants $p_{\min}>0$ and $\eta\in(0,1/2)$,
\begin{align}
P(Z=z)\ge p_{\min},
\qquad
\eta\le P(A=1\mid Z=z)\le1-\eta.
\end{align}
The balance metric uses a bounded characteristic kernel $k$ on the joint space of $(X,W)$. Approximate results additionally require the outcome-relevant stability condition introduced below. This stability condition is not implied by injectivity and is not generally identified from one proxy.

\subsection{Observable Joint Balance}

For a fixed representation and retained stratum $z$, let
\begin{align}
R_{a,z}\triangleq\mathcal L(X,W\mid A=a,Z=z),
\qquad
p_z\triangleq P(Z=z).
\end{align}
The stratum discrepancy and its aggregate are
\begin{align}
\Delta_z(\phi)
&\triangleq
\operatorname{MMD}_k(R_{1,z},R_{0,z}),\\
D(\phi)
&\triangleq
\sum_{z:p_z>0}p_z\Delta_z(\phi).
\end{align}
Because $k$ is characteristic,
\begin{align}
D(\phi)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
(X,W)\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid Z.
\end{align}
Exact balance is therefore an observable population property of a constructed representation. It is not assumed to be attainable.

\subsection{Exact Bias Decomposition}

Let $Q_{a,z}\triangleq\mathcal L(X,U\mid A=a,Z=z)$, let $\nu_{\phi,z}\triangleq Q_{1,z}-Q_{0,z}$, and define the latent outcome regression $\mu_a(x,u)\triangleq\mathbb E\{Y(a)\mid X=x,U=u\}$. If $\pi_z\triangleq P(A=1\mid Z=z)$, then the population bias has the exact decomposition
\begin{align}
\tau_\phi-\tau
=
\sum_{z=1}^M p_z
\int
\left\{(1-\pi_z)\mu_1(x,u)+\pi_z\mu_0(x,u)\right\}
d\nu_{\phi,z}(x,u).
\label{eq:bias-decomposition}
\end{align}
Equation~\eqref{eq:bias-decomposition} identifies the object that proxy balance must control: outcome-relevant functionals of the latent treatment-arm contrast, rather than every latent direction.

\subsection{Approximate Balance and the Stability Certificate}

The proxy channel sends a latent signed contrast $\nu$ to an observable kernel embedding. Denote this embedding operator in stratum $z$ by $J_{\phi,z}\nu$. For treatment level $a$, denote the corresponding outcome functional by
\begin{align}
\ell_{a,\phi,z}(\nu)
\triangleq
\int\mu_a(x,u)d\nu(x,u).
\end{align}
Outcome-relevant stability requires a common finite constant $\Gamma$ such that, over the prespecified representation class and admissible latent contrasts,
\begin{align}
|\ell_{a,\phi,z}(\nu)|
\le
\Gamma\|J_{\phi,z}\nu\|_{\mathcal H_k}.
\label{eq:uniform-stability}
\end{align}
Combining \eqref{eq:bias-decomposition} with \eqref{eq:uniform-stability} yields the established population certificate
\begin{align}
\boxed{|\tau_\phi-\tau|\le\Gamma D(\phi).}
\label{eq:population-certificate}
\end{align}
The constant $\Gamma$ is a sensitivity parameter unless outside information calibrates it. A global RKHS outcome bridge with norm at most $\Gamma$ is a sufficient structural witness for uniform stability, but the canonical method does not estimate that bridge.

\subsection{Exact Identification and Attainability}

If stability holds and $D(\phi)=0$, then \eqref{eq:population-certificate} gives $\tau_\phi=\tau$. Under proxy-channel invariance and conditional mixture injectivity, exact joint balance also implies $U\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid X$; together with $X\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid Z$, latent exchangeability, consistency, and overlap, this yields
\begin{align}
Y(a)\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid Z
\end{align}
and identifies the ATE by adjustment for $Z$.

Identification at an attained endpoint must be separated from existence of that endpoint. For the class of all maps from $X$ to at most $M$ strata, primitive strict overlap implies the established equivalence
\begin{align}
\exists\phi:D(\phi)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
W\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid X
\quad\text{and}\quad
e(X)\ \text{takes at most $M$ values},
\end{align}
where $e(X)\triangleq P(A=1\mid X)$. Thus exact balance is a strong certification result, while the practically interesting confounded regime is generally approximate.

\subsection{When Discrepancy Orders Realized Bias}

The bound in \eqref{eq:population-certificate} does not generally imply that a smaller discrepancy produces a smaller realized bias. Under the established restricted alignment result with binary severity, an $x$-homogeneous informative proxy channel, an additive homogeneous outcome, within-stratum $X$ balance, and a common direction of latent selection, the proxy-marginal discrepancy satisfies
\begin{align}
|\tau_\phi-\tau|=\kappa D_W(\phi)
\end{align}
for a representation-independent $\kappa>0$. This identity gives order preservation inside that restricted model class. Outside it, the paper claims minimization of a certified worst-case bias, not automatic minimization of realized bias.

\subsection{Independent Audit-View Extension}

A proxy-augmented extension splits a high-dimensional measurement into a representation view and an untouched audit view, writes
\begin{align}
W=(W^{\mathrm{rep}},W^{\mathrm{audit}}),
\qquad
R=\rho(X,W^{\mathrm{rep}}),
\end{align}
and evaluates $(X,W^{\mathrm{audit}})\perp\!\!\!\perp A\mid R$. This prevents the representation from making audit balance tautological by copying the audited coordinate. Exact point identification is available only under additional multi-view conditional independence and an outcome-relevant null-space or injectivity condition for the audit channel. This extension is currently a conditional theorem direction, not part of the canonical established theory. Its approximate stability bound is also open and will not be presented as a proved result.
