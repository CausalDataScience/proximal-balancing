# ICLR 2027 submission checklist for PROBE

Source authority: the local snapshots in [`official/`](official/) and the official template in [`template/iclr2027/`](template/iclr2027/). Items below are preparation requirements, not evidence that a submission has been made.

## Abstract deadline — September 18, 2026, 23:59 AoE

- [ ] Submit a genuine, informative abstract; do not use a placeholder or duplicate abstract.
- [ ] Confirm the complete author set. Authors cannot be added or removed after the abstract deadline.
- [ ] Confirm that every author has an up-to-date OpenReview profile linked to the submission email.
- [ ] Confirm author quotas: no author may appear on more than 20 submissions, and the special cap for submissions without an eligible reciprocal reviewer is satisfied.
- [ ] Select the reciprocal-reviewing author, or choose and explain the applicable exemption.
- [ ] Confirm the abstract-deadline-locked OpenReview fields: author membership, reciprocal-reviewing author, recent qualifying paper, exemption choice, and exemption reason.
- [ ] Enter title, keywords, and abstract. The current OpenReview schema allows at most 250 characters for the title and 5,000 characters for the abstract.
- [ ] Consider a TL;DR of at most 250 characters; this field is optional in the current schema.
- [ ] Choose a primary area. `causal reasoning` is the likely candidate for PROBE, but the authors must make the final selection.
- [ ] Decide whether to request the optional Paper Assistant feedback. The invitation states that it consumes the submitting user's single voucher and ends at the abstract deadline.

## Full-paper deadline — September 25, 2026, 23:59 AoE

- [ ] Use the official ICLR 2027 LaTeX style without modifying its formatting rules.
- [ ] Keep the initial-submission main text to at most 9 pages.
- [ ] Place references after the main text; bibliography pages do not count toward the main-text limit.
- [ ] Place appendices after the references, either in the paper PDF or as separate supplementary material. Reviewers are not required to read appendices or other supplementary material.
- [ ] Keep the submission double blind. Remove author identity from the paper, appendix, code, links, file metadata, and supplementary material.
- [ ] Cite related papers by the same authors in the third person. Existing arXiv papers do not by themselves break anonymity.
- [ ] Upload a PDF no larger than 50 MB.
- [ ] If used, upload one self-contained supplementary `.zip` or `.pdf` no larger than 100 MB. Its deadline is the full-paper deadline.
- [ ] Keep the final title and abstract close to the genuine abstract submission. Title and abstract may be edited until the full-paper deadline.
- [ ] Finalize author order. Order may change until the full-paper deadline, but author membership may not.

## Required statements and acknowledgements

- [ ] Add a dedicated AI-use disclosure section to the paper. It does not count toward the page limit.
- [ ] Make the paper disclosure and the OpenReview `AI_assistance` selections accurate and mutually consistent.
- [ ] Review every AI-assisted claim, proof, experiment, code artifact, citation, and passage; the authors remain responsible for the full submission.
- [ ] Read and acknowledge the ICLR Code of Ethics for every author.
- [ ] Read and follow the ICLR Code of Conduct.
- [ ] Acknowledge the reciprocal-reviewing requirement, AI disclosure requirement, and other submission instructions in the OpenReview form.
- [ ] Acknowledge the OpenReview visibility policy: submissions become public during review, accepted and rejected papers are deanonymized after decisions, and withdrawn papers are deanonymized immediately.

## Recommended statements and reproducibility

- [ ] Include an Ethics Statement before the references if the work raises ethics questions. The author guidelines allow at most one page and exclude it from the page limit.
- [ ] Include a paragraph-length Reproducibility Statement before the references that points to assumptions, proofs, algorithms, data processing, code, and supplementary material. It does not count toward the page limit.
- [ ] Ensure every theorem has a complete proof in the paper or appendix and every empirical result has a traceable implementation and data provenance.
- [ ] Prepare anonymized code as supplementary material or through a genuinely anonymous repository if code is submitted.

## Policy checks

- [ ] Confirm that the work is not identical or substantially similar to a paper already published, accepted, or simultaneously submitted to another archival conference or journal.
- [ ] Confirm that all reciprocal-review eligibility claims are supported by accepted qualifying publications as of the abstract deadline.
- [ ] Confirm that related non-peer-reviewed preprints and workshop versions are cited and described accurately.
- [ ] Understand withdrawal consequences: after the full-paper deadline, withdrawn submissions remain public and are immediately deanonymized.
- [ ] Recheck the live official pages immediately before submission because policies and form fields can change after this packet's retrieval time.

## Final local QA before any future upload

- [ ] Compile with zero LaTeX errors using the official style.
- [ ] Review relevant warnings, cross-references, citations, figures, tables, equations, and bibliography.
- [ ] Render and visually inspect every page for margins, page count, clipping, font substitution, and anonymity leaks.
- [ ] Scan the PDF and supplementary archive for author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, non-anonymous URLs, repository metadata, and hidden file metadata.
- [ ] Verify the upload artifacts' hashes and sizes.
- [ ] Obtain explicit user authorization before interacting with OpenReview or sending any external message.
